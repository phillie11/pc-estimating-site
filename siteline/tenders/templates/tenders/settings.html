How these are used

Bid/no-bid check. Moving a lead to Tendering asks six weighted questions and gives a Go, Review or No-go. At or above this value (the tender value, or the client's budget if there's no value yet) the questions must be answered; below it the estimator can skip them. At 0, the default, every bid needs them, and a bid with no value or budget always does. If a skipped bid's value later goes over the line, it is asked again before it moves to Submitted.

Quotes wanted per trade. On each tender's coverage board, and the company-wide one, a trade with fewer quotes in than this is shown red.
