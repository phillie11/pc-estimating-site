The job's CDM 2015 record: who the duty holders are, whether it is notifiable to HSE, the construction phase plan and the key dates.

1. Add the client, principal designer and principal contractor. The client and principal contractor are filled in for you.
2. Under **HSE notification**, put the length of the job and the most people on site at once. The register says whether it looks notifiable; if it is, record the F10 reference and date.
3. **Upload the plan** before work starts. A new upload becomes the next version and the old one is kept.

The project manager gets a Needs me item from 14 days before the start on site if no plan is uploaded. Everything here is advisory: check your contract and your safety adviser.
