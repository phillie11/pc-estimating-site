Company-wide charts.

- **Tender workload** shows every open bid from enquiry to tender return, so you can see clashes before you take on another.
- **Secured work** shows every won and live job from start on site to completion.
- **Tender analytics** shows your win rate by number and value, and why recent bids were lost.
