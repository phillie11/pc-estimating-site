**Team** lists everyone in the workspace. Owners and admins invite staff and say whether each person works in estimating, projects or both. External people (clients, consultants and subcontractors) never use a paid seat.

**Settings** holds your company name, logo and brand colour, which appear in the portal and on every PDF, plus your defaults for cost control, subcontract payments and the diary.
