Reports across every job, for owners and admins. Each figure is read from the job's own page, so a report always agrees with it.

- **Portfolio margin**: each live job's forecast margin from its CVR, and how far it moved since last month. Pick a month to look back.
- **Cash position**: money in and out on each live job this month and the next two, from its cashflow.
- **Overdue across jobs**: RFIs, NEC clocks, payment notices we owe, client payments, permits and defects past their date. Click a reference to deal with it.
- **Safety**: incidents by month and kind for the last year, and how many were RIDDOR reportable.
- **Subcontractor performance**: submittals made on time, snags per package, and how long snags take to close.

*Export CSV* on each report takes the full table into a spreadsheet. Advisory: check your contract and your safety adviser.
