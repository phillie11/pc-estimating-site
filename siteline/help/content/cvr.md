The cost value reconciliation: what the job is worth against what it has cost, each month, with the forecast to the end.

1. **Start the month's CVR.** Value, cost, the forecast final account and the forecast final cost fill in from applications for payment, the cost ledger and the contract.
2. **Add accruals and adjustments**, each with a reason: costs incurred but not yet invoiced, under or over valuation, risks and opportunities. *Bring last month's forward* copies the ones that still count.
3. **Submit it for sign-off** once the month has ended. The figures are then fixed.
4. An owner or admin **signs it off**, and the month is locked for good. Anything found later goes in a later month as an adjustment.

The company CVR shows every job for a month on one page. Advisory: check your contract.
