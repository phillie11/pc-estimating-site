Your subcontractors: trades, regions, ratings, accreditations and insurance expiry dates.

- **Add subcontractor** needs only the company name. Add contacts and insurance when you have them.
- On a project, **Packages** lists each trade package with the quotes received and who is appointed. Invite a subcontractor to the portal from there in one click.
- A subcontractor with history is archived, not deleted.
