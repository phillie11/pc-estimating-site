Requests for information, numbered per project (RFI-001), with who has the ball and when the answer is needed.

## Raise an RFI
1. Press **Raise RFI**.
2. Write the question as the subject and add detail, a drawing reference and a photo if they help.
3. Choose who it's for and when you need the answer by. They're emailed and see it in their portal.

Mark it *internal* to keep it to our own team. Use **Export CSV** for the full register. **Waiting on me** shows the ones you need to answer.
