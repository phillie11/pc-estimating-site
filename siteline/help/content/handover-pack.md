Turn the bid's internal pricing workbook into the handover pack for the delivery team. **Internal only**: it shows cost, margin and buy-out targets, so only staff who see the job's commercial figures can open it.

1. On the bid's **Pricing** tab, choose the completed workbook (.xlsx) and press **Upload and check**. Every upload is a new revision, and the trade totals show what changed.
2. Siteline recalculates every line and checks the total against the pricing summary to the penny. Fix anything flagged in the workbook and upload again.
3. When the bid is won, the pack goes to the project manager under **Commercial → Handover pack**. Give each open item an owner and a date; it shows in their Needs me.
4. **Issue to the delivery team** once it reconciles. **Export to Excel** for the file.
