Turn the bid's internal pricing workbook into the handover pack for the delivery team. **Internal only**: it shows cost, margin and buy-out targets, so only staff who see the job's commercial figures can open it.

1. On the bid's **Pricing** tab, choose the workbook (.xlsx or .xlsm) and press **Upload and check**. An .xlsm's macros are removed; only that copy is kept. Every upload is a new revision.
2. Siteline recalculates every line and checks the total against the pricing summary to the penny. Figures it can't recalculate come from Excel, marked. Fix anything flagged and upload again.
3. Owners choose the workbook layout under **Settings → Pricing workbook**.
4. When the bid is won, the pack goes to the project manager under **Commercial → Handover pack**. Give each open item an owner and a date; it shows in their Needs me.
5. **Issue to the delivery team** once it reconciles. **Export to Excel** for the file.
