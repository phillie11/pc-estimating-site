One list of everything waiting on you, across every project: overdue first, then today, this week and later.

1. Open the top item. The button says what to do.
2. Once you've done it, it drops off this list by itself.

Use the filters to look at one kind of thing or one project. The number by the bell is the same count. You can choose how often you get the round-up email on *Your account*.
