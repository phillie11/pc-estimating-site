Record each hole you seal through a fire-rated wall or floor, so the building has evidence of how it was done.

**To record one:** press *Record fire-stopping* and say where it is. Add what passes through, the rating and the product used if you know them. Take a photo before sealing and one of the finished seal.

With a photo of the finished seal it goes straight to the site team to inspect and sign off. Otherwise open it later, add the photo and press *Mark as sealed*.

If a seal **fails inspection**, it shows here with what's wrong and is on your Needs me. Put it right, add a new photo and press *Mark as resealed*.
