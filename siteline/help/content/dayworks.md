Record work done on daywork on site, get it signed, and get it valued.

1. **Start a daywork sheet**: just say what the work was. The date is today.
2. **Add each person, item of plant and material**, with hours or quantity. Pick a rate from the job's list, or type it.
3. Add **photos** from the phone camera.
4. **Get it signed**: the client or contract administrator signs on your phone with a finger. Not on site? *Ask them to sign it in their portal*: it goes on their Needs me and you're told when they sign. They see the hours and quantities, never the rates.
5. **Add it to the change register**: one step makes a change event priced from the sheet.

The project manager or QS sets the job's daywork rates and percentage additions from the contract under *Rates*.
