Company-wide targets that put things on people's Needs me. Owners and admins change them.

**Safety:** how many days after an incident or near miss is reported it should be investigated (7 to start with), and how long visitors' QR sign-ins are kept (12 months to start with, 3 to 36). Visitors' names are deleted after that in a clear-out each night. Sign-ins by your staff and portal users, and incidents, are kept.

**Close-out:** how many working days the project manager has to give a client-reported defect to whoever will fix it (2 to start with). Weekends and bank holidays don't count.

Agree these with your safety adviser and whoever looks after your data protection.
