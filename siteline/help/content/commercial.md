The job's contract set-up and its change register. Only staff see this.

- **Contract**: pick the form (JCT or NEC4) and the standard terms fill in. Check them against the signed contract. Everything here is advisory.
- **Change register**: early warnings (EW-001) and change events (CE-001), priced as cost and revenue. A change event can become a client variation and sub variations in one step.
- The clocks shown, such as NEC4 time bars and quotation periods, are advisory. Check your contract.
