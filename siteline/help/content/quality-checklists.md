These are the checklists your teams inspect against on every job. Siteline starts you with typical fit-out checklists; change them to match your own specifications and quality plan.

**To add one:** choose *Add a checklist*, give it a name and type the checks, one per line.

**To change one:** open it and edit the lines. Inspections already started keep the checks they started with. Untick *In use* to retire a checklist without losing its history.

Project managers and admins can change the library; other staff can read it.
