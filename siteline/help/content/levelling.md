Put subcontractors' quotes on the same footing, line by line.

1. Type the scope lines in, or import them from a spreadsheet.
2. Enter each subcontractor's rate or sum per line. With AI drafting on, **Map with AI** reads their attached quote onto your lines as a draft; check the tie to their total, then accept line by line or all at once.
3. Plug anything missing in one click, at the average or highest of the others, and add exclusions and corrections with a reason.

The levelled totals, ranks and exclusions register show who is really cheapest. **Carry in tender** marks your choice. It tells nobody anything.
