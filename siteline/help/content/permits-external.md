Hot works, working at height, isolations and confined spaces need a permit to work before the work starts.

**To ask for one:** press *Ask for a permit*, choose the type, say what the work is and where, and check the times. Press *Send for approval*.

The site team checks the precautions and approves it, or says why not. You're told either way. **Work must not start until it is approved.**

Open a permit to see the precautions confirmed and when it runs out, and press *Print permit* for the site board. When the work is finished, tell the site team so they can close it.
