Close-out takes a job from practical completion to the end of the defects period.

- **Record practical completion** for the whole works, or add a section for sectional completion. The defects period end comes from the contract and reaches *Needs me* six weeks before. Advisory: check your contract.
- **Defects**: the client reports them from their portal with a photo. Give each to whoever will fix it, then check and close it. Snags raised after practical completion become defects automatically.
- **O&M manual**: set it up from your company's contents list, then ask each subcontractor for their section. It reaches them as a submittal.
- **Certificates**: upload test certificates and results as they arrive.
- **Handover pack**: build it, check it, then issue it to the client. Every file is listed with its checksum, so a copy can be checked later.
