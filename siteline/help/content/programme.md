The job's Gantt chart: activities with their dates, progress and milestones.

- Bars outlined in red are behind or out of sequence.
- The coloured line marks today.
- On a phone the chart scrolls sideways and opens with today in view.

Staff add lines with the form under the chart, and choose which lines the client and subcontractors can see.
