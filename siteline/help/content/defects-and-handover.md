This page is for anything that needs putting right after handover, and for your handover documents.

- **Report a defect**: say what's wrong and where, and take a photo if you can. The site team is told straight away.
- **Defects you have reported** shows how each one is getting on: reported, being fixed, being checked, then fixed and checked. You are told when one is done.
- **Handover documents**: download the O&M manual and the handover pack here once they are issued. Each file in the pack has a checksum, so you can confirm a copy is exactly what was handed over.
