One diary entry per job per day: weather, labour, plant, deliveries, visitors, instructions, delays and photos.

1. On site, open **Today on site** and use the big buttons to add each line as the day goes on.
2. Add photos straight from your phone's camera.

An entry can be edited on its day and the next working day, then it locks. The project manager can unlock it with a reason. If a working day has no entry by 10am the next day, it goes on the PM's Needs me.
