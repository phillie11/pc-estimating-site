Shop drawings, samples and product information for the site team to approve.

- Upload a submittal with its files. You'll be told the outcome.
- **A** is approved, **B** is approved with comments (go ahead, taking the comments on board) and **C** means revise and resubmit.
- To resubmit, open it and upload the new revision. The history is kept.
