Budget, orders, actual costs and the forecast final cost for this job, by cost code.

1. **Set the budget** once, from the tender value less your margin.
2. **Commit** each appointed package as an order, or add orders by hand.
3. Record **actual costs** as invoices and timesheets come in.

The report shows the forecast margin. Below the company's warning level it goes red and goes on Needs me. Mistakes are reversed, never deleted, so the ledger always adds up.
