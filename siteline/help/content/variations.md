Changes to the contract work, priced by us and approved or rejected by the client in their portal (VO-001).

1. Press **New variation**. A title is enough to start; add the price and any time impact when you have them.
2. When it's ready, submit it to the client. They're told and decide in their portal, with their PO reference.
3. Approved variations add to the forecast final account.

Tick *proceeding at risk* if work has started before approval. **Export CSV** gives the full register.
