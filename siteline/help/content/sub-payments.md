Applications from subcontractors (SA-001), our payment notices and payments, CIS and retention.

1. Applications arrive from the subcontractor's portal, or you record one for them.
2. Give **our payment notice** in time. If we miss it, the application stands as the sum due, shown in red.
3. Record the **payment**. CIS is worked out from the subcontractor's status, and the cost goes to the job's cost ledger.

CIS figures are for your own return. Nothing is filed with HMRC from here. Dates are advisory.
