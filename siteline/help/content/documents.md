The drawing register for this job, numbered to ISO 19650. The current revision is the highest one, not the last uploaded.

## Add drawings
1. Press **Bulk upload** and choose the files. Numbers and revisions are read from the file names.
2. Check the preview, then add them to the register.

## Issue them
Issue revisions on a **transmittal** to the people who need them. That shares them, and each person acknowledges receipt. **View** opens a PDF in the browser, where you can pin a snag or RFI to a spot on the drawing.
