Record every hole through a fire-rated wall or floor, so the building has evidence of how each one was sealed.

**To add one:** choose *Add a penetration* and say where it is. Add what passes through, the rating, the product and who sealed it now or later. To pin it on a drawing, tap and hold the spot in the viewer and choose *New fire-stopping*.

**Photos:** take one before sealing and one of the finished seal. Adding the finished photo marks it as sealed.

**Sign-off:** sealed penetrations, including those subcontractors record in their portal, are on the project manager's Needs me. Whoever marked a seal done can't sign it off (Settings, Quality). Sign it off, or fail it with a note of what's wrong so it gets resealed. A resealed or reopened one needs a new photo first; a signed-off one changes only after the PM reopens it.

*Export CSV* gives the whole register for the handover pack.
