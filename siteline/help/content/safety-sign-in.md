Who is on site now, from the QR poster at the site entrance.

1. Open **QR poster**, print it and put it up where everyone passes.
2. People scan it with their phone camera. Anyone with an account in your company signs in with one tap; visitors type their name and company. They scan again to sign out.
3. **On site now** shows everyone signed in today. Use **Sign someone in** for anyone without a phone, and **Sign out** when they leave.

In a fire, open **Fire roll call** and tick people off at the assembly point. Open it once while you have a signal and it keeps working without one. If a poster gets out to the wrong people, **Make a new QR code** and the old posters stop working.
