Our interim applications to the client (AP-001).

1. Press **New application**, enter the work done to date and check the variations and materials included.
2. Retention, previous payments and VAT are worked out for you from the contract.
3. Submit it. The client is told and gives their payment notice in the portal.

The Construction Act dates (due date, payment notice, pay less notice, final date) are shown as advisory clocks. Check your contract.
