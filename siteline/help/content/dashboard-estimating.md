Your bids at a glance: what is in the pipeline, tender returns coming up, subcontractor quotes still awaited and anything waiting on you.

- Every number opens the list behind it, so you can check exactly what was counted.
- **Needs me** on the right lists what you personally have to do, soonest first.
- To start pricing a new job, press **New bid**. Only the name is needed; add the rest as you learn it.

Owners and admins also see the *Getting started* checklist here until the company is set up.
