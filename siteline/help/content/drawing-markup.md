Look at a drawing in the browser, draw on it, and see what changed between revisions.

- Pinch or use **+** and **−** to zoom, and drag to move around.
- **Mark up** opens the tools: pen, line, arrow, box, cloud, note, measure and rub out. Draw with a finger or the mouse; two fingers still zoom. Your mark-up saves itself.
- Your mark-up is private until you choose who sees it under **Mark-ups**, below the drawing. Tick other people's mark-ups to show them, even ones drawn on an older revision.
- **Export** the page with the ticked mark-ups as a picture or a PDF.
- **Compare revisions** shows two revisions side by side, or overlaid: red was taken out, blue was added, black is unchanged. **Cloud a change** marks it on your own mark-up of the newer revision.
- Measurements use the scale on the drawing's details. Check one against a written dimension before you rely on it.
