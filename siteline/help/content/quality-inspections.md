An inspection checks one area of the job against a checklist from your company's library, such as fire doors or partitions before boarding.

**To inspect:** choose *Start an inspection*, pick the checklist and say where you are. Then go down the checks on your phone and tap **Pass**, **Fail** or **N/A** for each. Open *Note or photo* to add what you found or take a photo.

**When a check fails:** tap *Raise a snag for this*. The snag goes to the subcontractor whose work it is, with your note and photo.

**To finish:** when every check is answered, tap *Finish inspection*. It is recorded as passed, or failed if any check failed. Only the project manager can reopen it.

Inspections you are doing are on your Needs me until they are finished.
