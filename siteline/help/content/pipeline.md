Your bids as cards, from Lead to Tendering, Submitted and Won.

- Drag a card to move it, or use **Move to** on the card (works on a phone and with a keyboard).
- Moving to **Won** opens the handover to the projects team.
- Column totals are the value of the bids in it. The weighted total uses each bid's probability.
