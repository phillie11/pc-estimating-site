The site team has asked you to sign a daywork sheet: a record of the labour, plant and materials used on extra work.

1. Press **Check and sign** and read the hours and quantities. Photos of the work are underneath.
2. Type your name, say who you are signing as, and sign in the box with a finger or mouse. No touch screen? Upload a photo of your signature on paper.
3. Tick to confirm you've checked it and press **Sign the sheet**.

Signing confirms what was used on the work. The value is agreed separately. If something looks wrong, don't sign: ask the site team to change it.
