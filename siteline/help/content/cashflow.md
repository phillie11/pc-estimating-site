Money in from the client and out on costs, month by month, actual to date and forecast to the end of the job. All excluding VAT.

- **Actual**: payments received, and actual costs on the cost ledger.
- **Forecast**: what is owed on applications already made, then the rest of the final account and final cost spread over the programme (or evenly to completion), paid on the contract's and the subcontract terms, and retention coming back.

Below the line means the job needs funding. The table under the chart has every figure; *Export CSV* takes it away. Advisory: check your contract.
