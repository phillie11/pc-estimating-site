Send a trade package out to subcontractors to price.

1. Add the packages for each trade under **Sub quotes**, then invite subcontractors. They get a link to the portal, never attachments.
2. Issue tender documents and addenda there. Answer their queries once for everyone.
3. Compare the quotes side by side, then **Level quotes** to put them on the same footing before you carry one in your tender.
