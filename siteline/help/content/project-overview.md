The front page of one job: where it stands, what's waiting, and the latest from the team.

- The tabs along the top are the job's registers. Anything past the eighth is under **More**.
- **Team** is where you invite the client, consultants and subcontractors. Their login is free, and they only see what you share with them.
- **Edit project** changes the details. A project with records on it can only be archived, never deleted.
