Snags (IS-001) are defects to put right. Tasks are jobs for someone to do.

- Staff raise a snag with a photo, a location and who is to fix it.
- The person it's assigned to fixes it and marks it **Ready for inspection**.
- The site team checks it and closes it, or reopens it with a note.

You only see the snags and tasks assigned to you, unless you're on the site team.
