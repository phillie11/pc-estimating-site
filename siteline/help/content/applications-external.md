An application for payment from the contractor.

- Check the figures and supporting files.
- Give your **payment notice** by the date shown, stating the sum you consider due.
- If you intend to pay less than a sum already notified, give a **pay less notice** before its deadline.

The dates shown are advisory. Check your contract.
