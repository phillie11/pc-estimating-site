Every bid or job you can open, with filters for open bids, live jobs, finished and (for owners and admins) archived ones.

## Add a project
1. Press **New project** (or **New bid**).
2. Give it a name. Everything else, such as client, value and dates, can wait.
3. Save. It opens on its overview, ready for documents, packages and the team.

## Won a bid?
Move it to *Won* on the pipeline or the project. You'll be asked for the handover: who runs it, the contract sum and the dates. The job then appears under Projects for the site team.

Projects with anything on them are archived, not deleted.
