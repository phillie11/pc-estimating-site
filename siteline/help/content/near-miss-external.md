Tell the site team about a near miss or an incident on this job. It takes under a minute. **If someone is badly hurt, call 999 first.**

1. Say what happened and where. The time is filled in as now.
2. Say whether anyone was hurt, and take a photo if you can.
3. Press **Send report**. The site team is told straight away and looks into it.

Reports from your company are listed underneath, so you can see the site team has them.
