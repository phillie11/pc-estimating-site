AI drafting is **off** until an owner or admin switches it on under **Settings → AI drafting**, with a monthly budget and who may use it.

What is sent to the AI service (Anthropic), and nothing more:

- **Clarifications**: workbook lines (trade, description, quantity, status, comment), provisional sums, exclusions, gaps, site rules. Supplier names, emails, phone numbers and amounts in comments are removed.
- **Bid brief and RFIs**: the PDFs you choose and the project name; for RFIs also existing RFI subjects, and the brief's risks if ticked. Your company profile goes with the brief.
- **Quote mapping**: that one quote, our scope lines and its typed total. No other bidder's prices.
- Your playbook for that draft.

Anthropic says data sent through its API is not used to train its models without the customer's express permission.

AI RFIs stay internal drafts until you issue them. Check every draft against its source.
