AI drafting is **off** until an owner or admin switches it on under **Settings → AI drafting**, with a monthly budget and who may use it.

What is sent to the AI service (Anthropic), and nothing more:

- **Clarifications**: each workbook line's trade, description, quantity, status and comment; provisional sum and allowance amounts; excluded trades, gaps, site rules, existing clarifications. Supplier names, emails, phone numbers and amounts in comments are taken out.
- **Bid brief**: the PDFs you choose, the project name, your company profile.
- **Quote mapping**: that one subcontractor's quote, our scope lines for the trade and the quote total typed in Siteline. No other bidder's prices.
- Your playbook for that draft.

Anthropic says data sent through its API is not used to train its models without the customer's express permission.

Every draft is marked as AI. It can be wrong: check each line against its source before you accept it.
