**RAMS**: subcontractors upload their risk assessments and method statements. The site team approves them, approves with comments, or asks for a revised version. The full history is kept.

**Sub variations**: a subcontractor submits a change with its price. The site team approves it in full or in part, rejects it, or passes it to the client as a draft variation.
