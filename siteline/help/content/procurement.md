**Schedule**: one line per package or long-lead item. Enter the date it's needed on site, the lead time and the approval period, and the *order by* and *submittals due* dates are worked out for you, avoiding weekends and bank holidays. Red means the order-by date has passed.

**Submittals** (SUB-001): shop drawings, samples and product data from subcontractors. Review each one and give **A** approved, **B** approved with comments, or **C** revise and resubmit. You can forward one to the designer first.
