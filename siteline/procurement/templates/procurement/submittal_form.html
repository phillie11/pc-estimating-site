What happens next

If it's on an appointed subcontractor's package, they're told and it shows under Submittals to make in their portal with the date it's due. With no package (a direct order), it waits on the reviewer to upload it.

When it arrives you have the job's review period to give it A (approved), B (approved with comments) or C (revise and resubmit). You can send it to the designer for comment first.

A C sends it back for revision 01, 02 and so on; every revision is kept. Once everything on a procurement line is A or B, the line moves to Approved.
